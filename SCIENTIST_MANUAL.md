# SCIENTIST_MANUAL.md
## Quick Guide for WIHG Scientists

This guide covers everything you need to review internship/dissertation requests, approve students, and clear them for their completion certificate.

---

## 1. Logging in

Your account is created by the Training Cell — you don't self-register. You'll receive your email and a temporary password; log in at the portal URL and change your password on first use (via your institution's usual account-change process, or ask the Training Cell to reset it).

---

## 2. Your Dashboard

After logging in you'll land on **Scientist Dashboard**, with three sections:

1. **Pending Requests** — students who selected you directly (or were pre-contacted) and are awaiting your decision.
2. **Awaiting Your Sign-off** — students who have finished their tenure, submitted their final report, and need your confirmation before a certificate can be issued.
3. **All My Students** — a full list of everyone assigned to you, with their current status.

---

## 3. Approving or disapproving a request

For each entry under **Pending Requests** you'll see the student's name, programme type (Internship/Dissertation), college, proposed duration, and topic (if given).

- Click **Approve** if you're willing to supervise them. This moves them to the fee-payment stage and automatically emails the student with payment instructions.
- Click **Disapprove** if you cannot take them on. This notifies the student automatically so they can look elsewhere or request auto-allocation instead.

There's no undo — double-check before clicking. If you approve someone by mistake, contact the Training Cell to have the application reassigned.

---

## 4. Confirming training completion (sign-off)

Once a student's tenure is complete, they'll submit their final report/thesis and a feedback form through the portal. Their entry will appear under **Awaiting Your Sign-off**.

1. Click **View final report** to review their submission.
2. If the work is satisfactory, click **Confirm Completion**.

This does *not* generate the certificate itself — it tells the Training Cell that you've verified the student completed their work satisfactorily. The Training Cell will then run the actual certificate generation.

**Important:** Only sign off once you're satisfied the report reflects genuine, completed work. Your sign-off is a required checkpoint before any certificate can be issued.

---

## 5. Frequently asked questions

**A student says they submitted their report but I don't see it.**
Ask them to check their dashboard shows "Certificate Requested" status — if it still shows "In Progress", their submission didn't go through and they need to resubmit.

**I approved the wrong student / need to change someone's duration or topic.**
These details are set by the student at application time and can only be corrected by the Training Cell. Contact them directly.

**Can I supervise more students than my listed seat count?**
Seat counts are managed by the Training Cell for auto-allocation purposes. Contact them if you'd like your available seats adjusted.

**Where do I see students who found me before creating an account?**
The "Connect with a Scientist" inquiry emails go straight to your inbox — they aren't tracked as formal applications until the student submits the Application Form and either names you directly or you approve them after they apply.
